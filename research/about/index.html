About & research

Philosophical questions for educational technology.

My work connects philosophy, AI ethics, educational technology, and academic integrity, with particular attention to reasoning, agency, trust, and human judgment.

Academic integrity, assessment, student reasoning, and the role of AI in learning.

Educational technology

Designing learning experiences that support inquiry, reflection, and accountable judgment.
