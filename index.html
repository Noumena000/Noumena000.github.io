Scholar · Educator · Builder

Philosophy is the connective tissue.

My work begins with questions about reasoning, judgment, agency, knowledge, and human flourishing. I teach applied ethics and critical thinking in higher education, write about AI and intellectual responsibility, and build software that translates those philosophical commitments into working systems.

The recurring design question is simple: how can intelligent systems increase human capacity without quietly displacing the judgment, responsibility, and reflective agency they are meant to support?
