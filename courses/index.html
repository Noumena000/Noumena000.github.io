Courses

Courses on thinking well.

Self-paced, interactive courses on critical thinking and reasoning in an age of AI. Each course runs in your browser, needs no account, and keeps your work on your own device.

Pilot · Version 0.1 (draft)

Thinking Well with AI

A critical thinking course for everyone: eight short sessions on judging claims, evidence, and AI answers, each with a hands-on lab, practice, a short dialogue, and a closing capstone.

Draft · Version 0.1

Thinking Well: Critical Thinking, Philosophically

A twelve-module course on reasoning well, taught the way philosophy is taught: arguments, evidence, bias, sources, disagreement, and values. Each module has practice with model answers, a push-back prompt, and a self-check, and the course ends with a capstone project.

About the pilot

Honest limits

This course is a draft that is still being reviewed and tested. It teaches these skills, but it has not yet been shown to improve anything. It cannot replace a doctor, lawyer, teacher, or financial adviser, and examples marked “invented” are made up for teaching.

Your progress and writing stay in your own browser. You can download your record at any time, and nothing you write is sent to a server. Feedback is welcome through LinkedIn.
