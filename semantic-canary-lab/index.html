A fair interpretation

A distinctive term may prompt a useful conversation about sources and reasoning. By itself, its appearance cannot establish where it came from, what tools someone used, who authored the work, or whether a course rule was broken.

Consider ordinary alternatives, including course materials, accessibility and translation tools, peer discussion, prior exposure, and independent wording. Explain the concern and give the learner a meaningful opportunity to respond.

Use the observation to support learning. Do not use a phrase match alone to change a grade, accuse a learner, or make a misconduct referral.

03 · Faculty facilitation

Turn uncertainty into inquiry.

Suggested discussion

Invite faculty to name what was observed, separate it from assumptions about how it happened, and identify what additional context would matter. Then practice an open, nonpunitive question that helps a learner explain and evaluate their reasoning.

Research and teaching limits

This Lab is a teaching illustration of a proposed methodology. It reports no controlled results, detection accuracy, validation, or student-level findings. Any research involving learners requires appropriate institutional, ethics, privacy, and accessibility review.
