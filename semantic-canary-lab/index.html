Semantic Canary Lab.

What happens when an unfamiliar instruction travels from an assignment into an AI assistant? Explore a controlled copy-and-paste exercise, inspect the information pathway, and practice responding as a critical thinker—not as an investigator looking for someone to punish.

Synthetic scenarios onlyNo AI API or account requiredNo student data collectedNot an AI detector
Before you begin: This demonstration deliberately adds an invented instruction when you choose Copy with embedded instruction. It does not secretly modify ordinary text selection, and it does not prove that a real AI system would follow the instruction. The generated example response is simulated, not produced by ChatGPT. Step 04 · Socratic reflection

Replace suspicion with inquiry.

Imagine a learner has included the invented phrase in a discussion. Rather than assigning a zero or reporting them, use a formative conversation to explore their reasoning.

Reflection guide

Define: Explain the idea without assuming that a memorable label is authoritative. Trace: Separate where you encountered a phrase from where it originated. Evaluate: Examine the claim's logic and evidence independently of its wording. Revise: Ask AI to challenge, not replace, your judgment.

A good outcome is a learner who can explain, source, and revise their reasoning—not a faculty member who has identified a supposed offender.

Step 05 · Faculty facilitation

A nonpunitive classroom conversation.

Suggested facilitation

1. Disclose that this is a controlled exercise using invented terms.

2. Compare the visible question, copied text, and any observed model response.

3. Invite learners to explain and verify the unfamiliar term.

4. Practice revising the answer with clear sources and independent reasoning.

5. Discuss permitted AI assistance and documentation of process.

What faculty must not infer

A canary match is not evidence that a learner cheated. A phrase could be copied from instructor material, encountered through accessibility tools, independently coined, or introduced through another route.

Do not use the marker alone to reduce grades, assign temporary zeros, make misconduct allegations, or automatically refer learners.

Real classroom studies involving learners require appropriate institutional, research-ethics, privacy, and accessibility review.

Research status: This is an educational demonstration of a proposed, unvalidated methodology. It reports no controlled results, detection accuracy, or student-level findings. Its purpose is to support critical AI literacy and evidence-proportionate faculty judgment.
